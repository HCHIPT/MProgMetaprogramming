## Scope of Materials

Python:
 - Introspection: Examining the type or properties of objects at runtime.
 - Reflection: Modifying the structure and behavior of objects at runtime.
 - Decorators: Modifying the behavior of functions or classes.
 - Dynamic Code: Generating and executing code dynamically.
 - Metaclasses: Customizing class creation and enforcing design patterns.

C++:
 - Reflection library (C++26): Inspection of program's structures during compile-time
